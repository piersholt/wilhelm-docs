# `0x24` Property Text: Radio

Radio `0x68` → GT `0x3b`

Updates the title substring previously defined by `0x23`.

### Related

- `0x23` [Title Text: Radio](23.md)
- `0x21` [Menu Text: Radio](21.md)
- `0xa5` [Body Text: Radio](a5.md)

### Example Frames

    68 0A FF 24 40 00 20 39 36 2E 30 C8     # " 96.0"
    68 0A FF 24 40 00 20 39 36 2E 31 C9     # " 96.1"
    68 0A FF 24 40 00 20 39 36 2E 32 CA     # " 96.2"

## Radio Values

Common format: `0x24` [Property Text](../common/24.md).

### Layout

Analogue-radio property updates use:

    ANALOGUE = 0x40

### String

The text replaces the substring marked by `0x03` and `0x04` in the preceding `0x23` title.

This allows the radio to update a frequency or similar changing property without rewriting the whole title.
