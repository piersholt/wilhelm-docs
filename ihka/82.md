# `0x82` IHKA Status

IHKA `0x5b` → Global Broadcast `0xbf`

Reports climate-control status used by the navigation/display system.

### Example Frames

    5B 04 BF 82 01 63
    5B 04 BF 82 03 61
    5B 04 BF 82 83 E1

### Related Commands

- `0x83` [IHKA A/C Control](83.md)

## Parameters

At least one data byte.

    REAR_WINDOW_DEFROSTER = 0b1000_0000

### Rear Window Defroster `0b1000_0000`

Indicates that the heated rear window is active.\
The MK4 does not seem to actually use the state though.
