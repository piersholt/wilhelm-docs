# `0x32` DSP Volume

Radio `0x68` → DSP `0x6a`

The radio uses the same direction/step encoding as the BMBT and MFL volume controls when applying volume changes to the DSP amplifier.

### Related Commands

- `0x32` [BMBT Volume](../bmbt/32.md)
- `0x32` [MFL Volume](../mfl/32.md)
- `0x36` [Radio EQ / DSP Control](../rad/36.md)

### Example Frames

    68 04 6A 32 11 25   # Up 1 step
    68 04 6A 32 30 04   # Down 3 steps
    68 04 6A 32 31 05   # Up 3 steps

## Parameters

Fixed length. One byte bitfield.

    DIRECTION   = 0b0000_0001
    STEPS       = 0b1111_0000

### Direction `0b0000_0001`

    DIRECTION_DOWN    = 0b0
    DIRECTION_UP      = 0b1

### Steps `0b1111_0000`

Number of volume steps to apply.

The BM54 splits larger internal volume changes into repeated messages with a maximum of three steps per frame.
