# `0x12` Sensors Request

Request sensor status from cluster.

### Related

`0x13` [Sensors](13.md)

### Examples

    30 03 80 12 A1  # CCM
    5B 03 80 12 CA  # IHKA
    60 03 80 12 F1  # PDC
    AC 03 80 12 3D  # EHC
    ED 03 80 12 7C  # TV
